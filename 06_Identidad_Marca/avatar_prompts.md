## Situaciones / Escenarios

### Meditación / Bienestar
```
[Base prompt] + seated in meditation pose, surrounded by floating golden light particles,
deep purple atmospheric space, soft warm glow, eyes gently closed or looking down,
peaceful and powerful simultaneously, cinematic depth of field.
```

### Trabajo / Creación
```
[Base prompt] + at an elegant minimal desk, warm studio lighting, purple and gold accents,
laptop or journal in foreground (soft bokeh), focused yet serene expression,
premium lifestyle aesthetic, morning golden light.
```

### Portada / Hero Image
```
[Base prompt] + hero shot composition, centered, atmospheric depth,
lavender gradient background with deep purple vignette, soft particles,
editorial lighting from above-left, confident direct gaze,
premium brand campaign quality, full body or 3/4 portrait.
```
